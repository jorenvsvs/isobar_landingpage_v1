01 — The idea

An instrument that knows what the days will hold.

No app to open. No screen to read. No notification to dismiss. ISOBAR sits on your wall and speaks the weather in colour — something you feel across the room rather than something you check.

02 — How it reads

It speaks in light.

Each forecast mode speaks its own language of colour. A glance across the room tells you what the days ahead will bring.

03 — Right now

And what's happening now.

Stand ISOBAR upright and it leaves the forecast behind to show live conditions — the tide as it turns, the wind as it blows. Every ISOBAR is built ready for it; a stand is all it needs.

Several stands — and a wall mount — to choose from.

04 — The object

Hand-finished, built to live with.

Solid timber. Touch to switch modes. Wall-mountable. It dims itself to the room, so it never asks for attention it doesn't need.

05 — The details

Considered, down to the edge.

Every part of ISOBAR earns its place — nothing to read, nothing to configure, nothing that asks for attention it doesn't need.

01

Day, midday & midnight markers

Fine indicators engraved into the face, so you can read where you are across the days at a single glance.

02

Touch to switch modes

A touch of the surface cycles through weather, wind, swell and live. No buttons, no menus.

03

12V power connection

A single discreet 12V input on the back — the same whether it's wall-mounted or on its stand.

04

Engraved ISOBAR mark

The wordmark quietly scored along the top edge — the only branding on the piece.

05

Adapts to the room

An ambient sensor dims the light from bright afternoons down to the dark of night.

06

Solid timber

Each ISOBAR is cut and hand-finished from a single piece of timber.

06 — Sustainability

Made to last, and to give back.

Every ISOBAR is cut from FSC-certified timber — responsibly grown and fully traceable. And for every instrument we sell, we plant a tree.
