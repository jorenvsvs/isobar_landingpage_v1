Section 3 · The concept

Now, here's how it works.

ISOBAR shows the forecast as a band of colour. Time runs left to right across the face — the next several days at a glance. The colour at each point tells you the conditions expected at that moment.

Calm / fairBuildingStrong / stormy
Today+1+2+3+4

No app to open, no screen to read. It sits on a desk or hangs on a wall, and one glance across the room tells you what the days ahead will hold. Tap it to switch between wind, weather and swell — or stand it upright for live conditions right now.
